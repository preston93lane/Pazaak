Grelko's Den

How to play

Goal: get closer to 20 than your opponent without going over. Win 3 sets to win the match.

Your turn: you're dealt a green card (1–10) from the main deck. Then you may play one card from your hand. Finish with End Turn (you'll get another card next turn) or Stand (lock in your total).

Bust: if you end your turn over 20, you lose the set.

Exactly 20 makes you stand automatically. Fill all 9 slots without busting and you win the set right away.

When both players stand, the closer total wins. Tied sets don't count, unless someone played a Tiebreaker. The loser of a set goes first in the next one.

Side deck: before each match you choose 10 cards. 4 are dealt at random into your hand and must last the whole match. Once you play a card, it's gone.

Collecting: you start with basic + and − cards. Beat bots or other players to earn packs. Duplicates past 3 turn into credits, which you can spend at Grelko's Den on powerful cards.
