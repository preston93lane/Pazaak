# Pazaak

The card game from *Knights of the Old Republic*, in one HTML file. Open `index.html` on a phone or computer.

- **Vs bots:** four cantina opponents from Easy to Champion. Works offline.
- **Online:** create a game, send the invite link, and each player picks a 10-card side deck before the match. It uses the same Firebase room setup as the Golf game.
- **Collecting:** you start with basic +1–6 and −1–6 cards. Win to earn packs. A card you already own 3 of turns into credits, which you spend at Grelko the Hutt's shop on rare and legendary cards.
- Your collection is saved in the browser on each device.

## Rules
Get closer to 20 than your opponent without going over. First to 3 sets wins. Each turn you're dealt a card from 1–10, then you may play one card from your hand, then End Turn or Stand. Exactly 20 stands you automatically. Filling all 9 slots without busting wins the set. A tie doesn't count, unless one player played a Tiebreaker. Your 4-card hand is dealt from your side deck once per match.
